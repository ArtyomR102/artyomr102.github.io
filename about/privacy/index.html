About privacy

Ah, so you wanna know more about how I collect and use your data. That's very thoughtful of you, actually, I wish more people cared about this stuff. You can check the privacy policy "set forth below" if you want to. And for now, let me explain things in a nutshell, to skip all the boring stuff.

Some info

It's common to divide all cookies on the Internet into 4 types:

  • Strictly necessary – without these, the site simply won't work. They're needed for login, encryption, and other things. These are purely technical and exist on almost any website.
  • Functional – these are used to store settings. Such cookies act like simple storage and usually don't share your data. This site doesn't have them yet, but they may appear here in the future.
  • Analytics/Statistics – now these cookies are used to collect data. I use a LiveInternet counter, which uses this kind of cookie. Still, you can disable them in your browser settings, and all the statistics collected are public and anonymous.
  • Targeting/Advertising – these guys don't just collect personalized data – they also sell or share it with third parties. You're not gonna find such cookies on my site.

What I do collect

  • Your IP address, which any site can see at any time
  • User agent (thing that mostly tells about your OS and browser type)
  • Your route through the site (current page, previous page)
  • Timestamp when you visited the page, and whether you're new here
  • Other minor technical things (screen resolution, for some reason?)

What I do not collect

  • Your personal info (full name, phone number, email)
  • Passwords, typed in or saved in password manager
  • Any sensitive data (social security, bank card details, etc.)
  • Your browsing history (god forbid)
  • Any files on your device
  • Access to your webcam and mic
  • Your favourite food and music taste

About selling data

I do NOT sell any users data to any third parties, how did you even think about that. Moreover, I hate the idea itself.

Privacy policy

(Effective date: 06 May 2026)

This Privacy Policy describes how this website (hereinafter referred to as "the Site", "I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and discloses information about visitors when they access the Site. 1. Information Collected

When you visit the Site, certain technical information is automatically collected by the LiveInternet counter (provided by LiveInternet.ru). This information includes:

  • IP address – used for approximate geolocation (city level) and to count unique visitors.
  • User agent string – identifies your browser type, operating system, and device.
  • Referrer URL – the address of the previous page you visited before arriving at the Site.
  • Current page URL and title – the specific page you are viewing and its title (up to 150 characters).
  • Screen resolution and color depth – technical parameters of your display.
  • Timestamp – date and time of your visit.
  • Visit status – whether you are a new or returning visitor (determined by a cookie set by LiveInternet).

No additional information is actively collected by me.

2. Cookies

The LiveInternet counter places a single cookie on your device solely for the purpose of distinguishing new visitors from returning ones. This cookie:

  • Does not contain personal data.
  • Is not shared with third parties for advertising or tracking purposes.
  • Can be deleted or blocked through your browser settings.

Additionally, your browser may store other cookies as part of its normal operation. The Site does not control those.

3. Use of Information

The information collected is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Counting the number of visitors, page views, and unique visits.
  • Analyzing general trends in browser usage, operating systems, screen resolutions, and referral sources.
  • Displaying public statistics via LiveInternet's "rating" and "counter" features (where applicable).
  • The statistics generated are publicly accessible by design of the LiveInternet service. Anyone can view the aggregated data for the Site.

The information is not used for:

  • Targeted or behavioral advertising.
  • Creating personal profiles of individual users.
  • Selling or transferring data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

I do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. The only recipient of the data is LiveInternet.ru, acting as a third-party analytics service. Their handling of data is governed by their own terms and policies, which are not fully published. No other third parties have access to the raw log data.

5. Data Retention

LiveInternet retains log data for an indefinite period as part of their statistical aggregation. I do not have the ability to delete individual records. If you are concerned about this, you may disable or block the LiveInternet counter using your browser settings (see Section 6 below).

6. Your Choices

You have several options to limit or prevent data collection:

  • Disable cookies in your browser – Most browsers allow you to block all cookies, including those used by counters. Refer to your browser's help documentation.
  • Use the "noCounter" URL parameter – You may append ?noCounter to any page URL on this Site. The counter will not load, and no data will be sent.
  • Use browser extensions – Privacy-focused extensions (such as uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger, or Ghostery) can block LiveInternet counters entirely.
  • Do not use the Site – This is the most drastic option, but always available.
