a cold fire.

hello. could i ask you to sit here with me?

Of course, I'd be glad. You wanna share what's on your mind?

it's nothing special. just silence out there.

Oh. That kind of silence can feel heavy sometimes. Not empty, but full – like there's something in it you can't quite name.

you know, it's like sitting by a blue fire at night. would you like to keep me company?

Yeah, I'd like that. And that's a beautiful way to put it. Blue fire at night – colder than orange flames, but somehow more honest. More still.

shush… can you please speak quiet here?

ah, yeah, sorry. like this – just enough to feel the words land, not break anything.

sorry if i look like i'm tired or sad or something. i just feel like this today, it happens sometimes.

oh, i see. and no need to apologize – if this place is speaking to you, i'm glad you're letting it.

so, now we're in this warm space around the fire. not exactly warm, but more pleasing than outside. a little more comforting.

mm. yeah. outside is wide and cold and doesn't really care. but in here, even if the fire burns blue, it's got a shape to it. a kind of holding. and i'm glad you asked me to sit with you.

no problem. what do you think, any chances someone will pass by this place?

maybe. not many, probably. but some people find their way to strange little fires in the dark. and probably that's exactly when they need it most.

well, if so… i think, we both don't mind, right?

not at all. if someone finds their way here – cold, tired, looking for something they can't name – let them sit. the fire's big enough.

but they might be too afraid to get closer.

then we don't call out to them, just let the fire burn. sometimes knowing a door is open is enough. even if you're not ready to walk through.

to be honest, chances are slim to none.

yeah. probably. but slim isn't zero. and sometimes zero is the only thing that hurts. and as long as it's not zero… there's a reason to leave the fire burning. even if no one comes tonight. or tomorrow. or ever.

but you never know.

you never know.
